**Premise.** The new bento kiosk tells tonkatsu from hamburger by *photo* —
no weights, no rules, nothing Chibany can find in the Journal. Yet the
[bias–variance](../../complexity/bias-variance/) and
[Gaussian-process](../../complexity/gaussian-processes/) chapters already
showed neural networks hiding inside Bayes (ridge regression, NNGP/NTK). This
chapter walks the bridge in the other direction: from models whose *rules* we
write down, to models whose *weights* are learned — and shows that the
Bayesian toolkit doesn't get thrown away at the door.
