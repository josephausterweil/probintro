**Premise.** The cafeteria recommender favors some dorms. But *which* fairness
does Chibany demand? Demographic parity, equalized odds, and calibration are
all **conditional probability statements** — a direct callback to
[Conditional Probability](../../foundations/conditional-probability/) — and
the impossibility result says you cannot have them all at once. Fairness turns
out to be a modeling *choice*, stated in the language this book taught.
