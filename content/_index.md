# A Narrative Introduction to Probability

**Probability, probabilistic machine learning, and probabilistic computing —
told as one school year in the life of Chibany, the Chiba Tech mascot.**

![chibany laying down](images/chibanylayingdown.png)

No prior math background required. Every idea arrives scene-first: a concrete
bento mystery, then the math it forces, then runnable GenJAX code.

## The book at a glance

| Part | Chibany's year | You learn |
|---|---|---|
| **I. [Foundations](foundations/)** | *Will there be tonkatsu today?* | events, counting, conditioning, Bayes' rule |
| **II. [The Tools](genjax/)** | teach the laptop to imagine days | GenJAX: simulate, trace, condition, infer |
| **III. [Continuous Probability & Bayesian Learning](learning/)** | the Mystery of the Two Peaks | densities, Gaussians, Bayesian updating, mixtures |
| **IV. [Structure](structure/)** | the Bento Provenance Project | generalization, Bayes nets, causality, information, hierarchies |
| **V. [Chains, Walks & Sampling](sampling/)** | the question you can't sum | Markov chains, random walks, MC, particle filters, MCMC |
| **VI. [Decisions & RL](decisions/)** | Chibany learns to act | decision theory, MDPs, Q-learning, inverse RL, POMDPs |
| **VII. [Model Complexity](complexity/)** | the Winter Model Fair | bias–variance, Bayesian nonparametrics, Gaussian processes |
| **VIII. [Deep Networks](deep/)** | the kiosk & the trainee | neural nets, transformers, LLMs & in-context learning |
| **IX. [Ethics, Fairness & Safety](ethics/)** | the Mascot's Code | adversarial ML, fairness, bias, alignment |
